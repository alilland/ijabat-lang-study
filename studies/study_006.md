## 183 ISO 639-1 Language Study

Starting Data: [/data/categories.yaml](/data/categories.yaml)

The following study rather than limiting the study to only the languages present on the list who are responsible for the highest Christian persecution, this study will expand to all the 183 ISO 639-1 languages. The ISO 639-1 language list provides standardized two-letter codes for the most widely used languages around the world.
